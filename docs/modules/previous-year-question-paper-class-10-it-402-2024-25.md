## <u>Section B: Subjective Type Questions</u>

### Answer any 3 out of the given 5 Employability Skills questions

### 6. Briefly explain the meaning of any 2 C's of effective communication.

**Answer:**

- **Clear/Clarity:** Say exactly what you mean and avoid confusing language.
- **Concise/Conciseness:** Use simple words and include only necessary information.

### 7. What is Stress Management?

**Answer:**

Stress management means finding ways to handle stress and stay mentally and physically healthy. It helps us deal with daily stress triggers and face challenges effectively.

### 8. Give any four tips to keep a computer in perfect working condition.

**Answer:**

- Uninstall unused programs and apps.
- Run disk-cleaner software.
- Keep the keyboard and screen free from dust.
- Keep software and apps updated.
- Install licensed antivirus software.
- Do not overcharge the battery.

### 9. Mention any four main functions of an entrepreneur.

**Answer:**

- Taking decisions about what to produce, how to produce and where to sell.
- Managing the business and arranging resources such as raw materials, money and workers.
- Handling finances, including production costs, salaries and rent.
- Taking risks and facing possible losses when starting something new.

### 10. Explain any two problems related to Sustainable Development.

**Answer:**

- **Food:** Fertile land needed for crops is being reduced because more land is used for other purposes.
- **Water:** Fresh water from rivers and ponds is used for drinking and cleaning, while waste and garbage are dumped into them.
- **Fuel:** Excessive cutting of trees for fuel and construction affects climate and natural resources.

### Answer any 4 out of the given 6 questions

### 11. Explain the Frame Style category in Writer.

**Answer:**

Frames are containers that can hold text, graphics and lists. Frame Style allows a document to be organised into sections, with each section having a different appearance.

### 12. What is a Macro? Give any two macro/module naming rules in Calc.

**Answer:**

A macro is a set of instructions that can execute recorded actions repeatedly to automate tasks.

- Begin the name with a letter.
- Do not use spaces.
- Do not use special characters except `_`.

### 13. What is the main purpose of Goal Seek in Calc? Give an example.

**Answer:**

Goal Seek is a data-analysis tool used to find the input value that produces a desired target result.

For example, it can calculate how many units must be produced to achieve a required output or profit.

### 14. What are the important prerequisites for setting a relationship between two tables in LibreOffice Base?

**Answer:**

- Both tables must have a common column.
- The common field must have the same data type in both tables.

### 15. Give any two health and safety requirements for using a keyboard.

**Answer:**

- Position the keyboard comfortably and separate it from the screen to avoid arm and hand fatigue.
- Maintain proper posture and wrist position to prevent strain.

### 16. Give any two eye-related problems caused by long computer use.

**Answer:**

- Eye strain and visual fatigue caused by screen brightness, glare and flickering.
- Dry eyes caused by blinking less while continuously looking at the screen.
- Computer Vision Syndrome caused by prolonged use, poor lighting and glare.

### Answer any 3 out of the given 5 questions

### 17. (a) What is the benefit of grouping drawing objects in Writer?

**Answer:**

Grouping allows multiple drawing objects to behave as a single entity without changing their individual size or position. A change applied to the grouped object affects all its members.

**(b) Differentiate between text wrapping and image alignment in Writer.**

**Answer:**

- **Text wrapping:** Controls how text is placed in relation to an image.
- **Image alignment:** Controls the horizontal or vertical placement of an image with respect to its anchor.

### 19. Fire safety and emergency handling awareness for employees.

**(a) Give any two fire-prevention measures.**

**Answer:**

- Know the location of fire extinguishers and how to use them.
- Clearly mark fire extinguishers and first-aid stations.
- Never block exits, extinguishers, electrical switches or panels.

**(b) Give any two basic emergency-handling procedures.**

**Answer:**

- Provide clear instructions about electrical and workplace safety.
- Keep a first-aid kit quickly accessible.
- Keep a list of emergency telephone numbers available.

### 20. Database relationships.

**(a) Give any two advantages of relating two tables.**

**Answer:**

- It helps maintain referential integrity.
- It prevents data redundancy.
- It prevents related data from becoming inconsistent.

**(b) Explain a many-to-many relationship.**

**Answer:**

In a many-to-many relationship, multiple records in one table correspond to multiple records in another table.
