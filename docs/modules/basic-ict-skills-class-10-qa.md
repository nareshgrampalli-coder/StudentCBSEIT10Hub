## <u>Session 1: Basic Computer Operations</u>

### 1. What is ICT?

**Answer:**

**ICT** stands for **Information and Communication Technology**. ICT helps in sharing, receiving, processing, and storing information.

An **ICT device** processes, stores, and delivers information to others.

Examples include:

- Laptop
- Desktop
- Tablet
- Smartphone

### 2. What are the objectives of Information and Communication Technology? What are the advantages and disadvantages of ICT?

**Answer:**

The full form of ICT is **Information and Communication Technology**. Its goal is to bridge the gap between parents, educators, and students by encouraging sustainable, cooperative, and transparent communication.

**Advantages of ICT**

- Enhanced modes of communication
- Independent learning platforms
- Cost efficiency
- Enhanced data and information security
- Paperless work and elimination of paper usage
- Better teaching and learning methods
- Web-based Learning Management System (LMS) tools

**Disadvantages of ICT**

- Traditional books and handwritten methods are at risk.
- Managing courses online can be difficult.
- Teachers require experience to handle ICT.
- There is a risk of cyber-attacks and hacking.
- Technology can be misused.

### 3. What is the difference between hardware and software?

**Answer:**

A computer system consists of two main parts:

- **Hardware:** The physical part of a computer system that can be seen and touched.

  Examples include CPU, motherboard, hard disk, keyboard, mouse, and printer.

- **Software:** Programs that perform different tasks on a computer system.

  A program is a collection of instructions that helps the computer perform specific tasks.

  Examples include OpenOffice Base, spreadsheet programs, and presentation programs.

### 4. What is BIOS?

**Answer:**

**BIOS** stands for **Basic Input/Output System**.

It helps the computer identify peripheral devices connected to the system through self-testing and helps the computer load the operating system properly.

### 5. What are the different types of keys on a keyboard?

**Answer:**

The different types of keyboard keys are:

- **Function keys:** `F1` to `F12` are used for specific purposes.
- **Control keys:** `SHIFT`, `CONTROL (CTRL)`, `ALT`, `SPACEBAR`, `TAB`, and `CAPS LOCK` are control keys.
- **Enter keys:** `ENTER` or `RETURN` is used to enter a command or move to the next line.
- **Punctuation keys:** Include keys for colon (`:`), semicolon (`;`), question mark (`?`), single quotation marks (`‘ ’`), and double quotation marks (`“ ”`).
- **Navigation keys:** `END`, `HOME`, `PAGE DOWN`, `PAGE UP`, and the arrow keys move the cursor up, down, left, or right in a document.
- **Command keys:** `BACKSPACE`, `INSERT (INS)`, and `DELETE (DEL)`.
- **Delete keys:** `DELETE` and `BACKSPACE` remove text, characters, or objects.
- **Windows key:** Opens the Start menu.

### 6. What is the purpose of a mouse?

**Answer:**

The purpose of a mouse is:

- **Roll over or hover:** Moving the mouse over a file shows details about the file.
- **Click or double-click:** Used to select, open, or delete files and folders.
- **Drag and drop:** Used to move a selected file or folder to another location.

## <u>Session 2: Performing Basic File Operations</u>

### 7. What are files and folders in a computer system?

**Answer:**

- **File:** A collection of information. Different types of files store different types of information. Every file has a name and an extension that identifies its type.
- **Folder:** A container that stores or groups files.

## <u>Session 3: Computer Care and Maintenance</u>

### 8. How do you maintain a computer system?

**Answer:**

Simple ways to care for a computer system include:

- Keeping the device clean, including the keyboard, screen, and mouse.
- Handling the device carefully.
- Keeping the device cool.
- Avoiding battery overloading.
- Preparing a maintenance schedule.

**Daily maintenance**

- Clean the email inbox.
- Save important attachments in a folder.

**Weekly maintenance**

- Clean the keyboard, monitor, CPU, and printer.
- Back up data.

**Monthly maintenance**

- Delete unused photographs.
- Clean the Downloads folder.
- Uninstall unused programs.
- Run a full system virus scan.
- Run disk-cleaner software.

**Yearly or annual maintenance**

- Clean up social media accounts.
- Clean up the email contact list.
- Update the operating system.

### 9. How can computer performance be increased?

**Answer:**

Installing many applications can slow down a computer.

To improve performance:

- Uninstall unnecessary applications.
- Remove temporary files.
- Remove outdated antivirus software updates.
- Keep the system updated and maintained.

## <u>Session 5: Computer Security and Privacy</u>

### 10. What is a security break?

**Answer:**

A **security break** is the leakage of information stored in a computer.

Personal information can be lost or leaked in two ways:

- We may disclose personal information, account details, or passwords through unsafe internet connections or websites.
- A person may gain unauthorized access to our computer, for example, if we forget to log out at work.

### 11. What are threats?

**Answer:**

**Threats** are ways in which personal information can be released from a computer without our knowledge.

- **Theft:** Stealing information or hardware.

  - **Physical theft:** Stealing a desktop computer or laptop.
  - **Identity theft:** A hacker obtains personal information and uses it to impersonate another person or gain access to accounts.

- **Software piracy:** Using or distributing unlicensed and unauthorized copies of a computer program or software.

- **Viruses:** Computer programs that corrupt data or software applications and steal data.

  - **Worm:** Replicates and spreads to all files on a computer after an attack, making it difficult to remove.
  - **Trojan horse:** Appears to be a useful program but behaves like a virus after entering the computer and destroys data.

- **Online predator:** A person who uses the internet to build unhealthy relationships and may persuade a person into unlawful activity online.

- **Internet scams:** False offers claim that a person has won a large sum of money and request an initial deposit. Depositing money or using a credit card or online banking can cause financial loss and misuse of account information.

### 12. How can we protect our data?

**Answer:**

To protect data from theft and viruses:

- **Use strong passwords**

  - Make passwords difficult to guess.
  - Use a combination of small letters, capital letters, special characters, and numbers.

- **Install antivirus software and a firewall**

  Antivirus software and a firewall protect data from viruses and hackers.

- **Encrypt data**

  Keep information in an encrypted format so unauthorized users cannot access it.

- **Open only secure websites**

  Check whether the website address begins with `https://`. An address beginning with `https://` indicates a secure website.
