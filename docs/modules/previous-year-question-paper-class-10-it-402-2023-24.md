## <u>Section B: Subjective Type Questions</u>

### 6. How can cultural differences be a barrier to effective communication?

**Answer:**

People from different cultures may not understand one another's customs, values or communication styles. Stereotypical assumptions can also create misunderstandings and differences of opinion, making effective communication difficult.

### 7. Explain any two misconceptions about entrepreneurship.

**Answer:**

- Every business idea needs to be unique or special.
- A person needs a lot of money to start a business.
- Entrepreneurs are born, not made.
- Entrepreneurs always make a profit.

### 8. Discuss the significance of time-management skills.

**Answer:**

Good time management helps people remain focused and productive. It reduces procrastination, stress and work-life imbalance while helping them achieve goals and obtain better results.

### 9. Explain spam mails and how they should be handled.

**Answer:**

Spam mails or junk mails are unwanted commercial messages sent from unknown sources without the recipient's consent.

- Report or block spam senders.
- Delete spam regularly.
- Unsubscribe from unwanted newsletters.
- Use a third-party spam filter.
- Never respond to spam.
- Keep the email address private.

### 10. Mention any two ways to obtain clean and affordable energy.

**Answer:**

- **Solar power:** Uses sunlight and does not require burning coal or other non-renewable fuels.
- **Biogas:** Provides an eco-friendly alternative to natural gas.
- **Wind energy:** Uses wind turbines to generate electricity.
- **Hydropower:** Generates electricity using the force of running water.

### 11. Write the main steps to group drawing objects in Writer.

**Answer:**

1. Select one object.
2. Hold `Shift` and select the other objects.
3. Right-click and choose **Group → Group**, or use **Format → Group → Group**.

### 12. What is the purpose of consolidating data in spreadsheets?

**Answer:**

Data consolidation gathers and summarizes data from separate worksheets or workbooks into a master worksheet. It makes grouped analysis easier and allows the summary to be updated.

### 13. Differentiate between LAN and WAN.

**Answer:**

- **LAN:** Covers a limited area such as a home, school or office building.
- **WAN:** Covers a broad area across a city, country or multiple countries. The Internet is the best-known WAN.

### 14. What are the two parts of a cell reference to data on another sheet?

**Answer:**

A reference consists of:

1. The sheet name.
2. The cell address.

For example, `='Savings Account'.F3` refers to cell `F3` on the sheet named `Savings Account`.

### 15. Give any two benefits of relationships between database tables.

**Answer:**

- Prevent missing or inconsistent related data.
- Reduce data redundancy.
- Maintain referential integrity.
- Allow data sharing and centralized control.

### 16. Mention any two fire-safety rules for an organization.

**Answer:**

- Install fire escape plans and fire extinguishers.
- Conduct regular fire drills.
- Place smoke alarms and fire detection systems at appropriate locations.
- Keep the workplace a no-smoking zone.
- Provide regular fire-safety training.

### 17. Explain mail merge and give two benefits.

**Answer:**

Mail merge combines a standard document with personalised recipient data to generate letters, labels or emails in bulk.

- Saves time and effort.
- Creates personalised bulk communication.
- Reduces manual work and is economical.

### 18. Explain four types of spreadsheet hyperlinks.

**Answer:**

- **Cell reference:** Links to a specific cell.
- **Sheet:** Links to a sheet in the same workbook.
- **External file:** Links to a local or network document or picture.
- **Webpage:** Links to an external Internet page.
- **Mail and News:** Opens a pre-addressed email or news link.

### 19. Give four general guidelines for strong passwords.

**Answer:**

- Keep the password at least 12–14 characters where permitted.
- Use uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols.
- Avoid names, dictionary words, sequences and personal information.
- Do not reuse passwords across accounts.
- Use a random password generator where possible.

### 20. Explain Forms and Reports in OpenOffice Base.

**Answer:**

**(a) Forms:** A form provides an interactive, user-specified layout for entering, viewing and changing data in database tables.

**(b) Reports:** A report presents formatted and summarized data retrieved from tables or queries in a printable, readable form.

### 21. Answer the SQL questions for the Product table.

| PID | PNAME | PRICE | QUANTITY | CATEGORY |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| P01 | BOARD | 10000 | 2 | OFFICE |
| P02 | EYELINER | 700 | 50 | MAKEUP |
| P03 | PENCIL | 550 | 500 | SCHOOL |
| P04 | LAMP | 250 | 10 | DOMESTIC |
| P05 | BEDSHHEET | 450 | 45 | DOMESTIC |
| P06 | LAPTOP | 75000 | 15 | OFFICE |

**(a) Display names of products in the Domestic category.**

**Answer:**

```sql
SELECT PNAME
FROM Product
WHERE CATEGORY = "DOMESTIC";
```

**(b) Display product name and price when quantity is less than 50.**

**Answer:**

```sql
SELECT PNAME, PRICE
FROM Product
WHERE QUANTITY < 50;
```

**(c) Display price and quantity of Laptop.**

**Answer:**

```sql
SELECT PRICE, QUANTITY
FROM Product
WHERE PNAME = "LAPTOP";
```

**(d) Display name and quantity for products priced above 5000.**

**Answer:**

```sql
SELECT PNAME, QUANTITY
FROM Product
WHERE PRICE > 5000;
```
