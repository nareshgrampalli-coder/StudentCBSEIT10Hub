## <u>Section B: Subjective Type Questions</u>

### 6. What are the 7 Cs of effective communication?

**Answer:**

- **Clear:** Be easy to understand.
- **Concise:** Use only necessary words.
- **Concrete:** Provide specific facts and examples.
- **Correct:** Ensure accurate grammar and information.
- **Coherent:** Keep ideas logically connected.
- **Complete:** Include all required information.
- **Courteous:** Be respectful and friendly.

### 7. What is self-awareness? Why is it important?

**Answer:**

Self-awareness is the ability to recognize one's own emotions, strengths, weaknesses, habits and their effect on others. It helps individuals make better decisions, control behaviour and improve relationships.

### 8. Why is taking responsibility an important entrepreneurial quality?

**Answer:**

Taking responsibility helps an entrepreneur accept ownership of decisions and outcomes. It builds trust, encourages accountability, improves problem-solving and ensures that challenges are addressed promptly.

### 9. Why divide daily activities into "Must Do", "Should Do" and "Nice to Do"?

**Answer:**

This division helps prioritize important tasks, manage time and reduce stress. Essential work is completed first, followed by desirable activities, while optional tasks are attempted when time permits.

### 10. State four myths about entrepreneurship.

**Answer:**

- Every business idea must be unique.
- A person needs substantial money to start a business.
- Entrepreneurs are born, not made.
- Entrepreneurs always make a profit.

### 11. What are occupational hazards? Mention two types.

**Answer:**

Occupational hazards are risks in the workplace that can cause injury, illness or property damage. Examples include physical hazards such as falls and electrical hazards, and biological or chemical hazards.

### 12. What is referential integrity? Give one situation it avoids.

**Answer:**

Referential integrity ensures that a foreign-key value must refer to an existing primary-key record. It prevents adding or keeping an orphan record, such as an order linked to a customer that does not exist.

### 13. Differentiate between styles and templates.

**Answer:**

- **Styles:** Store formatting for particular text, paragraphs, pages or frames and can be applied repeatedly.
- **Templates:** Provide a complete reusable document layout and structure, often including styles, text and placeholder sections.

### 14. List purposes for creating spreadsheet hyperlinks.

**Answer:**

- Move to a specific cell or sheet.
- Open another workbook or external file.
- Open a webpage.
- Open a pre-addressed email message.
- Navigate quickly to frequently used information.

### 15. What is Scenario Manager? How does it help analyze outcomes?

**Answer:**

Scenario Manager stores and compares alternative sets of input values in a spreadsheet. It allows a user to switch between scenarios and observe how changing variables affects calculated results.

### 16. Which feature creates a contents list from document headings, and what must be done for it to work?

**Answer:**

Use **Table of Contents**. Apply the correct built-in heading styles to each heading and subheading. The feature can then generate the list of entries and page numbers automatically and update it after changes.

### 17. Define database reports and explain how to insert date and time.

**Answer:**

A report is a formatted, printable presentation of data retrieved from a table or query. To insert date and time in LibreOffice Base:

1. Open the report in Edit mode.
2. Place the cursor in the page header or another suitable report area.
3. Choose **Insert → Fields → Date** or **Time**, or insert the required date/time field.
4. Save and preview the report to confirm the result.

### 18. Write the SQL query for SDetails and explain WHERE and ORDER BY.

**Answer:**

```sql
SELECT *
FROM SDetails
WHERE FavouriteColour = 'Blue'
ORDER BY RollNo ASC;
```

`WHERE` filters rows whose favourite colour is Blue. `ORDER BY RollNo ASC` sorts the filtered records by RollNo in ascending order.

### 19. Explain the anchoring options in LibreOffice Writer.

**Answer:**

- **To Page:** Keeps the object at a fixed position on a page.
- **To Paragraph:** Moves with the paragraph to which it is attached.
- **To Character:** Moves with a particular character.
- **As Character:** Places the object within the text line like a character.

The chosen anchor controls movement when text is inserted or deleted.

### 20. Why is Solver more appropriate than Goal Seek, and what are its major setup steps?

**Answer:**

Goal Seek changes one variable to reach one target, whereas Solver can adjust several variables while applying constraints and maximizing or minimizing an objective.

Major steps:

1. Identify the target cell and desired objective.
2. Identify cells that may change.
3. Enter investment constraints.
4. Run Solver and review the solution.
5. Accept the result if it satisfies all constraints.
