## <u>Session 1: Stress Management</u>

### 1. To become successful in life, which basic self-management skills do you require?

**Answer:**

The basic self-management skills required are:

- **Self-awareness:** Request honest feedback, understand your personality and work-related skills, and consider how you handled situations and how you could handle them better.
- **Responsibility:** Accepting responsibility and ownership is important for self-development.
- **Time management:** Make a list of tasks, prepare a schedule, and follow it.
- **Adaptability:** Stay updated on best practices and new information. Prepare for changes so that you can make a smooth transition.

### 2. What is Stress?

**Answer:**

**Stress** is our emotional, mental, physical, and social response to perceived demands or threats. The pressures or threats are called **stressors**.

Examples include:

- Feeling unprepared a few days before examinations.
- Worrying about what others will think if you do not dress properly or speak confidently.
- Feeling stressed because of a lack of sleep.

### 3. What are the three steps to manage stress?

**Answer:**

The three steps to manage stress are:

1. **Be aware that you are stressed:** Look for symptoms such as headaches, insomnia, sadness, excessive worrying, and anxiety. Stress can be managed when it is identified.
2. **Identify the cause:** Find out the reason for your stress, such as examinations, family pressures, financial problems, or poor diet.
3. **Apply stress-management methods:** Use time-management tools, concentrate on important tasks, talk to a friend or family member, exercise, watch a movie, or do activities that help you recharge.

### 4. What are the different stress-management techniques?

**Answer:**

Some stress-management techniques are:

- Training managers
- Time management
- Organizing academic life and avoiding delay
- Maintaining a healthy diet
- Staying positive
- Doing physical exercise and getting fresh air
- Getting proper sleep
- Taking holidays with family and friends

### 5. What is emotional intelligence in self-management skills?

**Answer:**

**Emotional intelligence** is the ability to recognize and regulate your own emotions and understand the emotions of others.

- **Emotional awareness:** Recognizing and naming your own feelings.
- **Harnessing emotions:** Using emotions effectively for thinking and problem-solving.
- **Managing emotions:** Controlling your own emotions appropriately and helping others do the same.

### 6. How can emotional intelligence be managed?

**Answer:**

Emotional intelligence can be managed by following these steps:

1. **Understand your emotions:** Analyze your behavior and make a list of areas in which you need to improve.
2. **Rationalize:** Avoid quick judgments and think logically before reacting.
3. **Practice:** Stay calm through meditation and yoga.

## <u>Session 2: Self-Awareness – Strength and Weakness Analysis</u>

### 7. How can you identify your strengths and weaknesses?

**Answer:**

Use the following techniques:

**Finding strengths**

- Think about activities in which you are usually successful.
- Consider what others appreciate about you.
- Take time to identify the things you do well.

**Finding weaknesses**

- Identify areas where you struggle or find tasks difficult.
- Look at the feedback others usually give you.
- Accept feedback openly and accept your weaknesses without feeling low.

## <u>Session 3: Self-Motivation</u>

### 8. What are the types of motivation?

**Answer:**

There are two types of motivation:

- **Internal motivation:** Doing activities that make you happy, feel good, or stay healthy.
- **External motivation:** Working for recognition, respect, or appreciation.

### 9. What are the qualities of self-motivated people?

**Answer:**

The qualities of self-motivated people are:

- They know what they want from life.
- They are focused.
- They know what is important.
- They are dedicated to fulfilling their dreams.

### 10. How can you build self-motivation?

**Answer:**

The four steps to build self-motivation are:

1. Find out your strengths.
2. Set and focus on your goals.
3. Develop a plan to achieve your goals.
4. Stay loyal to your goals.

## <u>Session 4: Self-Regulation – Goal Setting</u>

### 11. How do you set goals in self-management skills?

**Answer:**

Goals can be set using the **SMART** technique:

- **Specific:** Set clear and specific goals.
- **Measurable:** Define how much or how many must be achieved and how you will know the result.
- **Achievable:** Break big goals into smaller parts so they are achievable.
- **Realistic:** Choose a goal that you genuinely want to achieve and can work towards.
- **Time-bound:** Set a timeframe within which the goal must be achieved.

## <u>Session 5: Self-Regulation – Time Management</u>

### 12. What are the benefits of time management?

**Answer:**

Time management is a thinking skill that helps you:

- Complete tasks on time.
- Submit homework and assignments on time.
- Estimate how long an activity will take.

### 13. What are the four steps for effective time management?

**Answer:**

The four steps are:

1. **Organize:** Make plans for daily activities.
2. **Prioritize:** Prepare a to-do list and arrange activities in order of importance.
3. **Control:** Control your activities and time.
4. **Track:** Keep track of how you spend your time.

### 14. How can I improve my time management?

**Answer:**

Improve time management by:

- Avoiding delay or postponement of planned actions.
- Organizing your room and desk at school.
- Creating a **“no disturbance zone”** to complete important tasks without interruption.
- Using waiting time productively.
- Preparing a to-do list.
- Setting priorities.
- Replacing ineffective activities with productive ones.
